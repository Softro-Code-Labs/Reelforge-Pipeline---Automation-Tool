# Privacy Policy — ReelForge

**Last updated:** 29 July 2026

ReelForge ("the App") is an automation tool that generates short videos and publishes them to
TikTok on behalf of the account owner who configured it. This policy explains what data the App
handles.

## 1. What we access

When you authorize ReelForge through TikTok Login, we request the following TikTok scopes:

- `user.info.basic` — your TikTok username and profile info, to confirm which account is connected.
- `video.upload` / `video.publish` — permission to upload and publish videos to your own TikTok
  account.

We do not access your followers' data, direct messages, or any other TikTok account's content.

## 2. What we store

- Your TikTok **access token** and **refresh token**, stored securely and used only to publish
  videos you (or the automation you configured) initiate.
- Locally generated video files, scripts, and captions created by the pipeline, stored temporarily
  for processing and debugging.
- We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties, except the service providers
  strictly necessary to generate content (e.g. Google Gemini for script generation, Pexels for
  stock footage), each of which only receives the minimum data needed to perform its function.

## 3. How long we keep data

Tokens are kept only as long as the App is actively connected to your TikTok account. Generated
video working files may be deleted automatically after each run. You can request deletion of any
stored data at any time (see Contact, below).

## 4. Your rights

You can revoke ReelForge's access to your TikTok account at any time from TikTok's app permissions
settings. Doing so immediately invalidates our stored tokens for your account.

## 5. Children's privacy

ReelForge is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to process data belonging to
children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
