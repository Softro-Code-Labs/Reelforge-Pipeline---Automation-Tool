How to upload manually

  1. Tap Download video above and save the .mp4 to your phone/computer.
  2. Open the TikTok app → tap + to create a post → Upload the downloaded video.
  3. On the caption screen, paste the Title and Caption text above into the caption box.
  4. Paste the Hashtags at the end of the caption (TikTok has no separate hashtag field — they just go inline in the caption text).
  5. Review the auto-generated cover thumbnail, then tap Post.
